Protocols Under Review

Before adopting a new or revised protocol, the Authority circulates the draft for review and comment by all significantly affected persons within the Berrien County EMS system, as required by MCL 333.20919(3)(a). Comments are accepted for 60 days from the posting date shown on each draft.

No protocols are currently posted for review.

Comments may also be submitted to comments@berrienmca.org. Identify the protocol by number and title, state your affiliation with the Berrien County EMS system, and describe the concern or suggestion.
